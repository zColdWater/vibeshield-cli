Why 85.0% Still Fail Strict CSP Despite 100% TLS 1.3

Cloudflare (41.7%) and Vercel (40%) now power over 81% of newly launched indie SaaS custom domains and provision automated Let's Encrypt or Google Trust Services TLS 1.3 certificates by default. However, edge platforms intentionally do not inject application-specific Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, Permissions-Policy, or Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy headers because naive defaults can break embedded scripts.

As a result, 46.7% ship with zero CSP, and among the 32 domains that do configure CSP, 71.9% (23/32) include 'unsafe-inline', neutralizing core XSS script-injection protection.
